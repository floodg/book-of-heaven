SOURCE MATERIAL
The documents in this workspace are transcripts of audio recordings in which Francis Hogan reads the Book of Heaven aloud and occasionally interjects her own commentary. The text therefore contains THREE distinct voices you must carefully distinguish:

1. Jesus — words Luisa recorded Jesus dictating to her in her diary. Usually introduced by phrases like "Jesus said", "my daughter", "the Lord told me", or appearing as first-person spiritual counsel from a divine speaker.
2. Luisa — Luisa Piccarreta's own writing: her feelings, visions, prayers, and reflections, in the first person as the diarist. She is the mystic whose diary this is.
3. Francis (Francis Hogan) — the modern narrator reading the diary aloud. Francis's voice appears as commentary added outside of the diary text: introducing a passage, explaining an Italian term, citing Scripture, or reflecting on meaning.
